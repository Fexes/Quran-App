# Purification and prayer guide research

Reviewed 21 October 2026. Covers `ghusl_content.dart`, `tayammum_content.dart`, `prayer_mistakes_content.dart`, `congregation_content.dart`, and `travel_illness_content.dart`: five guides, sixteen chapters. Each guide contains its full source list, and chapters reference those source IDs.

The practical sequences are explicitly Hanafi unless labelled otherwise. Shafi‘i comparisons explain concrete differences. The one-contact tayammum alternative is attributed to Shaykh Ibn Baz. These are original concise explanations, not copies of the source translations. The illustrations are supporting artwork, not proof of a legal ruling.

## Ghusl

Primary reports: [Bukhari 248](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:248), [282](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:282), [269](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:269), and [Muslim 330a](https://sunnah.com/muslim:330a).

Interpretation is anchored in [Faraz Rabbani’s Hanafi method](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/the-ritual-bath-ghusl-obligatory-recommended-and-disliked-acts/), [Darul Iftaa Chicago on intercourse](https://daruliftaa.us/fatwa/571/), [Shazia Ahmad on mouth and nose rinsing](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/is-it-obligatory-to-rinse-ones-mouth-and-nose-in-ghusl-and-wudu/), [Egypt’s Dar Al-Ifta on ghusl and wudu](https://www.dar-alifta.org/en/fatwa/details/24353/performing-ablution-for-prayer-after-the-ritual-bath-for-major-ritual-impurity), and [Ahmed Al-Ahmad on Shafi‘i essentials](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/purity-shafii-fiqh/what-are-the-things-that-require-ghusl-ritual-bath/).

Editorial decisions: distinguish pleasure from ejaculation, a dream from relevant discharge, and madhi from major impurity. Separate required coverage from recommended sequence. Mark intention and mouth/nose differences. Do not attempt a universal menstrual calendar or irregular-bleeding algorithm. Bismillah is recommended, not a spoken intention or an invented bath-specific supplication.

## Tayammum

Foundation: [Qur’an 5:6](https://quran.com/5/6). Hanafi material and invalidation rules were checked against [Mukhtasar al-Quduri, translated by Suheil Laher, PDF pages 16–17](https://data.nur.nu/Kutub/English/Laher_Quduri.pdf). Modern explanations: [Tabraze Azam’s method](https://seekersguidance.org/uncategorized/how-to-make-dry-ablution-tayammum/), [Faraz A. Khan on stone](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/performing-tayammum-if-paralyzed/), and [Egypt’s Dar Al-Ifta on usable water returning](https://www.dar-alifta.org/en/fatwa/details/23955/performing-tayammum-and-then-finding-water-before-the-prayer).

Comparison sources: [Darulfatwa’s Shafi‘i method](https://www.darulfatwa.org/islam-basics/tayammum-dry-purification/) and [Ibn Baz’s official Arabic transcript](https://ibnbaz.org/audios/333/باب-صفة-التيمم). Completed-prayer discussion uses [Abu Dawud 338](https://sunnah.com/abudawud:338), with an explicit reservation for school-specific repeat-prayer cases.

Editorial decisions: do not merge the schools’ rules for material, wrist/elbow extent, contacts, prayer-time entry, or reuse. Ordinary inconvenience is not presented as lack of usable water. The guide distinguishes available assistance from independent washing ability.

## Prayer mistakes

Hanafi repair examples: [Sulma Badrudduja on missed obligatory actions](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/forgetting-to-perform-an-obgligatory-action-of-the-prayer/), [Faraz Rabbani’s sujud al-sahw sequence](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/how-to-perform-the-forgetfulness-prostration/), and [Dawat-e-Islami, Method of Salah, printed page 97, PDF page 108](https://data2.dawateislami.net/Data/Books/Download/en/pdf/2006/71-1.pdf). The last source supports the bounded first-sitting example; intermediate rising positions have further detail and are not collapsed into a universal instruction.

Shafi‘i comparison: [Jordan Iftaa, Dr. Nidal Sultan, Rulings of the Prostration for Forgetfulness](https://www.aliftaa.jo/article-en/5953/Article.aspx?ArticleId=6011) and [Jordan Iftaa question 4431](https://www.aliftaa.jo/QuestionPrintEn.aspx?QuestionId=4431). The article URL was opened and verified by its displayed title and author; the transient CaptchaImage URL is not used. Hanafi doubt distinctions use [Salman Younas](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/doubts-in-prayer-making-up-such-prayers/).

Editorial decisions: a missing pillar is not repaired merely by two extra prostrations. The Hanafi after-salam method and Shafi‘i before-salam method are separately labelled. Ordinary count doubt and obsessive doubt are distinguished. The prostration remembrance is from [Muslim 772](https://sunnah.com/muslim:772), not a special invented sujud al-sahw formula.

## Congregational prayer

Primary reports: [Bukhari 722](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:722) and [636](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:636). Recitation comparison uses [Waseem Hussain’s Hanafi explanation](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/follower-reciting-behind-the-imam/), [Shazia Ahmad’s Shafi‘i guidance](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/shafii-fiqh/can-i-recite-the-fatiha-during-the-imams-fatiha-in-the-shafii-school/), and [Muhammad Afifi al-Akiti’s latecomer distinctions](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/shafii-fiqh/follower-reciting-al-fatiha-in-the-shafii-school/).

Late-joining details use [Naielah Ackbarali on catching a unit](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/a-latecomers-prayer-when-is-one-considered-to-have-missed-a-cycle-rakat/), [Tabraze Azam on recitation](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/latecomer-recitation-in-prayer/), [Faraz Rabbani’s worked sitting-order examples](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/the-rulings-related-to-a-latecomer-a-note-on-the-durr-al-mukhtar/), and [Tabraze Azam on the imam’s corrective prostrations](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/latecomer-imam-performing-prostrations-forgetfulness/).

Editorial decisions: all four makeup examples are Hanafi and retain both recitation and sitting order. Friday-prayer late arrival is explicitly outside those ordinary-prayer examples. The Shafi‘i Fatiha exception is limited to a genuine latecomer, not deliberate delay.

## Travel and illness

Hanafi travel: [Faraz A. Khan’s basic rulings](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/the-basic-rulings-of-travel/), [Tabraze Azam on distance estimates](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/when-should-i-start-shorten-my-prayers/), [the actual route beyond the city](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/calculate-distance-two-cities-know-traveler/), and [Faraz Rabbani on the intended stay](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/can-i-shorten-my-prayers-if-i-stay-less-than-15-days-in-each-town/). Shafi‘i comparison uses [Shuaib Ally](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/shafii-fiqh/considered-traveller-shafii/). Applying a combining concession consistently is supported by [Nail Kandi](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/prayer-shafii-fiqh/for-how-many-days-can-a-hanafi-combine-his-prayers-as-a-traveler/).

Illness: [Bukhari 1117](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1117), [Abdurrahman al-Kharsa’s Hanafi posture distinctions](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/how-should-a-sick-person-pray/), [Yusuf Weltch on lying down and head gestures](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/can-i-pray-laying-down-due-to-sickness-and-surgery-if-so-how/), and [his illness-related combining discussion](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/is-it-permissible-for-the-elderly-to-combine-prayers-in-hanafi-school/).

Editorial decisions: 77 km is a labelled Hanafi estimate, not universal consensus. Stay periods are tied to school and intention. Shortening and combining are distinct. Illness changes feasible postures, not the prayer’s unit count. The seated and bed examples retain the Hanafi distinction between physical prostration and head gestures; complete inability to gesture is identified as a separate case.

## Validation scope

Source titles, author attribution, scripture references, and chapter-to-source links were reviewed. All linked sources are scripture reports, a classical legal manual, named scholarly answers, or institutional guidance. No tests or simulators were written or run for this content task. Native visual verification and independent scholar review are not claimed.
