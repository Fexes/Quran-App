# Worship and pilgrimage guide research

Reviewed 21 October 2026. The six new guides contain 18 illustrated chapters. They use concise original instructional prose, linked primary texts, institutional rulings, and named scholarly answers. Arabic prayer texts are traditional texts, with transliteration and concise meanings. This is an editorial research record, not certification by a religious authority.

| Guide | Chapters | Chosen scope |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Fasting | 3 | Ramadan preparation, fasting day, exemptions and missed days |
| Zakat | 3 | Adult cash, precious metals and trade wealth; calculation and payment |
| Jumu‘ah | 2 | Preparation, khutbah and the two-unit congregational prayer |
| Eid prayer | 2 | Morning preparation and explicit Hanafi/Shafi‘i prayer alternatives |
| Janazah prayer | 3 | A clearly labeled Shafi‘i example for an adult deceased person |
| Hajj | 5 | A visiting pilgrim’s Tamattu sequence, Umrah through farewell |

## Fasting

The framework is [Quran 2:183–185](https://quran.com/2/183-185) and [2:187](https://quran.com/2/187). Dawn is distinguished from sunrise. The nightly pre-dawn intention is a shared safe practice, without declaring other schools’ valid timing rules invalid. [Abi Dawud 2454](https://sunnah.com/abudawud:2454) supplies the pre-dawn narration; [Bukhari 1923](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1923) supports suhur.

Deliberate, involuntary and forgetful actions are separated. The Maliki make-up position for forgetful eating is stated alongside the majority, using [Dar Al-Ifta 6221](https://dar-alifta.org/en/fatwa/details/6221/things-which-invalidate-the-fast) and [Bukhari 1933](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1933). [Abi Dawud 2380](https://sunnah.com/abudawud:2380) and [Dar Al-Ifta 1220](https://www.dar-alifta.org/en/fatwa/details/1220/wet-dreams-during-the-fasting-hours-of-ramadan) support the involuntary examples. The former’s English translation says “atonement”; its Arabic uses qada, so the guide does not infer kaffarah from it.

The exact iftar remembrance is from [Abi Dawud 2357](https://sunnah.com/abudawud:2357), graded hasan on the source. Menstruation make-up comes from [Muslim 335c](https://sunnah.com/muslim:335c). Exemptions and differing remedies draw on [Dar Al-Ifta’s Book of Fasting](https://www.dar-alifta.org/images/Languages/OnlineBooks/Thebookoffasting.pdf), pp. 24–29. The guide avoids prescribing a universal penalty or a medical decision. Eid/Tashriq guidance cites [Bukhari 1991](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1991) and [1997–1998](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1997).

## Zakat

The 87.48 g gold/612.36 g silver convention is labeled Hanafi and supported by [SeekersGuidance’s calculator](https://seekersguidance.org/zakatcalculator/) and [Islamic Relief Worldwide’s FAQ](https://islamic-relief.org/zakat-faq/). The alternative 85 g convention is not labeled “pure gold”: [Dar Al-Ifta 7746](https://www.dar-alifta.org/en/fatwa/details/7746/zakah-on-collected-and-uncollected-debts) supplies that number without a purity specification. Dar Al-Ifta 4585, which specifies 21-carat gold, was deliberately not used to support a pure-gold claim.

The abstract example is 12,000 minus 2,000 eligible deductions, multiplied by 0.025, giving 250. It assumes nisab and hawl are met and does not use market prices. [Bukhari 1454](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1454) provides one fortieth on silver. [Faraz A. Khan’s Hanafi debt answer](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/paying-zakat-vs-paying-off-a-debt/) supports qualified deductions. Different treatment of jewellery, investments, debt and the hawl is retained rather than compressed into one universal formula.

[Quran 9:60](https://quran.com/9/60) supplies recipient categories. [Dar Al-Ifta’s intention ruling](https://dar-alifta.org/en/fatwa/details/23927/the-importance-of-forming-the-intention-at-the-time-of-paying-zakat) is cited for intention only; its broad interpretation of “Allah’s cause” is not presented as consensus. [Its maintenance-duty ruling](https://www.dar-alifta.org/en/fatwa/details/21918/covering-granddaughter%E2%80%99s-marriage-expenses-from-zakat-money) supports the warning to check family eligibility. [Bukhari 1503](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1503) distinguishes zakat al-fitr and its pre-prayer timing.

## Jumu‘ah and Eid

Friday’s congregational structure follows [Quran 62:9–10](https://quran.com/62/9-10), [Bukhari 883](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:883), [934](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:934), and [the Hanafi rulings edited from Ashraf Ali al-Thanawi](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/the-rulings-related-to-friday-prayer/). Exemptions, late completion, greeting prayer during the sermon, and supplementary prayers are qualified. [Dar Al-Ifta 4997](https://www.dar-alifta.org/ar/Fatwa/Details/15339/حكم-صلاة-الجمعة-خلف-التلفاز) directly supports the remote-broadcast distinction. A home two-unit prayer is not substituted for Jumu‘ah.

Eid shows the Hanafi three/three method from [Darul Iftaa Mahmudiyyah](https://daruliftaa.net/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/Eid-Guidelines.pdf), and the Shafi‘i seven/five method from [Jordan Iftaa article 3481](https://www.aliftaa.jo/Article.aspx?ArticleId=3481). Counts exclude the ordinary opening and rising takbirs. [BBSI’s official guidance](https://www.bbsi.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/BBSI0G8-Guidance-for-Eid.pdf), pp. 5–7, supports the four-school status comparison. These 2020 documents are used for stable ritual details, not their pandemic restrictions or operational recommendations. The prior Jordan status link was replaced after unreliable retrieval.

The Eid takbir wording is a recognized formula documented by [Dar Al-Ifta 7547](https://dar-alifta.org/ar/fatwa/details/20500/أقوال-العلماء-في-صيغة-التكبير-لصلاة-العيد-وما-عليه-الفتوى), not claimed as the sole prophetic wording. [Bukhari 953](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:953) supplies the dates practice; [962](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:962) places the sermon after prayer.

## Janazah

[Jordan Iftaa 3619](https://www.aliftaa.jo/QuestionPrintEn.aspx?QuestionId=3619) supplies the labeled Shafi‘i framework. Its imperfect English translation was checked against its actual intended rulings: knowing the deceased’s name is not required, and supplication after the fourth takbir is recommended rather than indispensable. The standing four-takbir structure is supported by [Bukhari 1333](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1333), with Al-Fatihah from [1335](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1335) and [Quran 1](https://quran.com/1).

The complete salawat Arabic matches [Bukhari 3370](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3370). The concise adult-male dua is the opening of [Muslim 963a](https://sunnah.com/muslim:963a); its feminine adaptation is explicitly described as adapted pronouns, not another narration. [Dar Al-Ifta 3094](https://www.dar-alifta.org/ar/fatwa/details/12565/حكم-التسليمة-الثانية-في-صلاة-الجنازة) supports the first/second salam distinction. Children, multiple deceased people and late joining are not treated as interchangeable with this adult singular example.

## Hajj

The guide explicitly chooses Tamattu. [Nusuk’s rites page](https://hajj.nusuk.sa/nusuk/hajj-rituals) supports distinctions from Qiran and Ifrad; [Quran 2:196](https://quran.com/2/196) supplies the hady and fasting alternative. Current access and service details point to [official Nusuk](https://hajj.nusuk.sa/), without fixing permits, eligibility countries, booking dates or fees in offline text.

The main chronology follows [Jabir’s account, Muslim 1218a](https://sunnah.com/muslim:1218a). [Ibn Majah 3015](https://sunnah.com/ibnmajah:3015) supports Arafat’s indispensable place. Talbiyah uses [Bukhari 1549](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1549); stopping at the first stoning is supported by [1685](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1685). The order on the 10th is qualified using [Bukhari 83](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:83) and [Ilyas Patel’s Hanafi hady answer](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hajj/can-i-exit-ihram-if-my-sacrifice-is-confirmed-online/). A receipt is not itself the sacrifice.

Tawaf al-ifadah, Hajj sa’i, release from ihram and timing qualifications are checked against [Dar Al-Ifta’s detailed rites](https://www.dar-alifta.org/en/article/details/102/the-rites-of-hajj-in-detail), [requirements](https://www.dar-alifta.org/en/article/details/50/the-requirements-of-hajj) and [Arabic Hajj guidance](https://www.dar-alifta.org/ar/haj). The completion sequence is sufficient across the cited schools; partial-release thresholds are not asserted to be identical. The 11th/12th stoning order and timing are supported by [Bukhari 1751](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1751) and [1746](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1746). Farewell and its menstruation exemption use [1755](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1755), without extending that exemption to ifadah.

Independent review prompted two explicit clarifications: Mina overnights are Sunnah in Hanafi law versus an obligation in the majority, supported by [Dar Al-Ifta 6341](https://dar-alifta.org/en/fatwa/details/6341/the-permissibility-for-women-and-the-weak-not-spending-the-night-at-mina). The majority’s sunset departure cutoff on the 12th differs from the Hanafi dawn cutoff on the 13th, documented in [Al-Azhar’s Hajj Guide](https://azhar.eg/Portals/0/dalil_hajj_2019.pdf), printed p. 77. Planning to leave before sunset remains practical advice, not a universal claim that missing it automatically has identical consequences.

## Editorial and integration checks

All chapter and supplication source IDs resolve within their own guide. The 18 new chapter IDs are unique. All referenced local images exist. Formatting and static analysis of the six content files pass. No tests or simulators were run. Contemporary sources are summarized briefly across topic-specific pages; long copied institutional descriptions and copied modern translations are avoided. The guidance preserves useful school qualifications without claiming to replace individual rulings for medical circumstances, unusual assets, interrupted rites or exceptional prayer situations.
