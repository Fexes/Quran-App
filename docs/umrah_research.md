# Umrah guide research

Sources checked: 21 October 2026. App copy is in `lib/features/learn/data/umrah_content.dart`. This is source-based editorial research, not a claim of independent scholarly certification. English meanings and transliterations are editorial renderings. The guide follows mainstream Sunni practice and acknowledges school-specific details.

## Structure

Six illustrated chapters: overview, ihram and miqat, tawaf, prayer and Zamzam, sa’i, and haircut/completion. This is an editorial sequence, not six legal pillars. Use “After tawaf” for the prayer chapter. The reference images supply visual direction only: Arafat and other Hajj-only rites must not become Umrah steps or illustrations.

## Evidence and decisions

| Overall rites, seven one-way sa’i lengths, haircut | [Official Nusuk journey](https://umrah.nusuk.sa/Journey) | Seven sa’i lengths end at Marwah. Remaining in ihram until haircut must be explicit. |
| Current booking and permits | [Nusuk Umrah](https://www.nusuk.sa/umrah) | Link current requirements, avoid freezing future eligibility or dates in offline text. |
| Current health requirements | [Saudi Ministry of Health](https://www.moh.gov.sa/en/healthawareness/pilgrims-health/pages/default.aspx) | Link the living official health page. Do not hardcode vaccine certificates or validity periods. |
| Miqat | [Bukhari 1524](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1524) | Boundary depends on route. Travellers must enter ihram before crossing; airport arrival is not a universal starting point. |
| Ihram preparation and restrictions | [Ministry Ihram Guide](https://haj.gov.sa/-/media/Project/HAJJ/Awareness-Guides/Ihram-Guide/English/EN-Ihram-Guide.pdf) and [Bukhari 1838](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1838) | Avoid the misleading shorthand “no stitching”. Men’s normal fitted garments are restricted, but sewn belts and bags are not categorically forbidden. |
| Talbiyah | [Bukhari 1549](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1549) | Full Arabic talbiyah, independently worded English meaning. |
| Mosque entry | [Muslim 713a](https://sunnah.com/muslim:713a) | Short authentic mosque-entry dua. Do not claim a special authenticated dua on first seeing the Kaaba. |
| Black Stone | [Bukhari 1613](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1613) | Gesture and takbir without crowding. Touching is not an Umrah validity condition. |
| Tawaf route | [Dar al-Ifta, duties of tawaf](https://dar-alifta.org/ar/fatwa/details/20651/بيان-واجبات-الطواف) | Counterclockwise, Kaaba on left, outside Hijr, seven complete circuits. |
| Counting, prayer placement, sa’i purity | [Alharamain guide](https://risala.prh.gov.sa/en/contents/114) | Use lower certain count when unsure; purity is recommended for sa’i, not a validity condition. |
| Dhikr during the rites | [Ibn Baz, fatwa 15716](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/15716/الدعاء-في-الطواف) | No fixed dua for each numbered circuit or length. |
| Right shoulder | [Abu Dawud 1883](https://sunnah.com/abudawud:1883) | The reported shoulder wrap is for tawaf, not for the whole Umrah journey. |
| Dua between corners | [Abu Dawud 1892](https://sunnah.com/abudawud:1892), graded hasan by al-Albani; [Quran 2:201](https://quran.com/2/201) | State the hadith grade accurately rather than calling every cited report sahih. |
| Wudu during tawaf | [Dar al-Ifta research](https://www.dar-alifta.org/ar/FatwaResearch/details/185/انتقاض-الوضوء-أثناء-الطواف) | Most schools classify purity as a validity condition; Hanafis classify it as obligatory. Instruct renewal, defer resumption and remedies to a qualified guide. |
| Two rak’ahs | [Muslim 1218a](https://sunnah.com/muslim:1218a) and [Islamweb 121305](https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/121305/ruling-of-two-rakahs-after-tawaaf) | Include the prayer. It is not universally “optional”: Hanafi and the well-known Maliki ruling require it for this tawaf. |
| Safa and Marwah dhikr | [Muslim 1218a](https://sunnah.com/muslim:1218a) | The complete traditional Arabic formula from Jabir’s account is included offline, with an original English meaning and transliteration. |
| Menstruation | [Bukhari 1650](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1650) | Normal rule is wait and purify before tawaf. Constrained travel requires individual advice, not a blanket invalidity or permission ruling. |
| Face covering | [Bukhari 1838](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1838) and [Jordan Iftaa 1259](https://aliftaa.jo/QuestionPrintEn.aspx?QuestionId=1259) | Distinguish fitted niqab/gloves from draped fabric. Do not say women can never cover their face in ihram. |
| Men’s footwear | [Islamic Portal, Yusuf Shabbir](https://islamicportal.co.uk/footwear-in-ihram/) | Acknowledge Hanafi difference regarding the top of the foot. “Anything below the ankle is allowed” is too broad. |
| Haircut scope | [Dar al-Ifta, Umrah rulings](https://www.dar-alifta.org/en/article/details/103/the-umrah-rulings-and-rites) and [Bukhari 1727](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1727) | Whole-head approach for men avoids presenting a school’s smaller minimum as universal. Women shorten, not shave. |
| Cutting one’s own hair | [Dar al-Ifta 6343](https://www.dar-alifta.org/en/fatwa/details/6343/the-ruling-for-a-muhrim-shaving-his-head-himself-or-for-another-after-finishing-t) | No invented requirement that the cutter must already have left ihram. |
| Haircut location | [Islamic Portal, haircut within the Haram](https://islamicportal.co.uk/does-hair-have-to-be-shaved-in-haram/) | Do not suggest waiting for a hotel outside the sacred boundary without qualification; the location matters in Hanafi practice. |

## Research limitations and source handling

The Ministry PDFs were discoverable through indexed official text, including the restrictions and preparations, but direct browser fetching returned HTTP 403. Accessible Nusuk pages, Alharamain guidance, original hadith texts and institutional fatwas were used to cross-check the practical sequence. Some official English pages simplify legal classifications; the cross-school qualifiers are deliberate. No automatic penalty calculator, ruling on a particular individual, or guarantee of religious acceptance is included.

Before a future content release, recheck the operational links and obtain qualified human review if the product intends to claim scholarly approval. No such approval is claimed by this implementation.
